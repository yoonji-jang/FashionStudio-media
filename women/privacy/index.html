Privacy Policy - @janggu.zip outfit tool

This app is a private tool used only by the owner of the @janggu.zip account. It creates outfit posts for the owner's own Instagram account.

What we access

With the owner's permission (scopes boards:read and pins:read), the app reads the owner's own Pinterest boards and pins to use saved pins as outfit references. It does not access other users' accounts and does not post, edit or delete anything on Pinterest.

How data is used and stored

  • Pin images are used only temporarily while a post is being made and are not kept afterwards.
  • Only pin IDs may be kept as references. No personal data of any Pinterest user is collected.
  • Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are kept in encrypted secret storage and are never shared.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest Settings > Security and logins > Apps.
